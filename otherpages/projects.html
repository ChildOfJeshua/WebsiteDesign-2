Youth Action Board T-Shirt Design

YAB T Shirt Design(s) Project that under one month was finalized to a set of designs including the set above this text. The group specifically requested the use of blue, black, and red, which are incorporated into the t-shirt graphics accordingly. I used their recommended site: RushOrderTees.com, as a visualization tool throughout the design process to preview how the final product would look. This helped ensure that the sizing, fonts, and color combinations worked harmoniously together. After several iterations and group approvals, this design was selected as the default version.

Drenched Logo Project

Drenched was more of a brand-focused project, where the client wanted the identity incorporated across media, clothing, and more, and my task was to elevate the provided line art reference. We incorporated a 3D-rendered symbol to add depth, applied color theory for stronger visual impact, achieved symmetrical alignment for a polished finish, and more. The design was transformed into the final draft shown on the left, before the client used ChatGPT to further refine it—resulting in the variation displayed on the right. With more changes and enhancements coming soon; there's bound to be further discussion to be held in other to best achieve another upgrade.
